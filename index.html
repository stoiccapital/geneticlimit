Natural Muscle Building

The genetic limit.

How much muscle a man can realistically build — and the hard natural ceiling — for every height from 155 cm to 230 cm.

The targets

  • Ideal body fat
    10–15 %
  • Realistic FFMI
    21–23 — what a lifetime natural lifter actually reaches.
  • Natural ceiling
    25 — the hard upper bound without drugs. Rare.

The mirror test

Skip the scales, calipers, and DEXA scans. Your bathroom mirror is the measuring instrument.

  • 10 % body fat
    Visible six-pack. All four rows of abs defined, obliques showing, veins on arms.
  • 15 % body fat
    Visible four-pack. Upper two rows defined, lower abs softer. Lean but not shredded.
